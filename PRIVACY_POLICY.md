# Privacy Policy for Hindi Cinema Movie Zone

**Effective Date:** September 20, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 20, 2026  

This Privacy Policy explains how **Hindi Cinema Movie Zone** ("we", "our", or "the App"), package name `com.southaction.hindimovies`, handles user information.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This mobile application is designed as a zero-friction movie discovery and playback curation tool: **we do not require an account, we do not track your identity, and we do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII).**

---

## 1. Information We Do NOT Collect

We believe in minimal data access. When you use the App:
- **No Personal Identifiers:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, billing details, or physical addresses.
- **No Authentication Required:** There is no registration, sign-up, or login required to access any feature of the App.
- **No Sensitive Permissions:** The App does not request access to your device's Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Call Logs, SMS, Photo Gallery, or Precise GPS Location.

---

## 2. Information Handled Locally on Your Device

### Local Watchlist & Preferences
- When you save movies to **"My List" (Watchlist)** or browse categories, this data is saved directly on your device using Android's local Room SQLite Database.
- This data **never leaves your device** and is never uploaded, synced, or transmitted to any developer server or external database.
- You have full control: you can remove items from your Watchlist at any time within the App, or wipe all saved data instantly via Android **Settings > Apps > Hindi Cinema Movie Zone > Storage > Clear Data**.

---

## 3. Device Permissions Used

The App requests only standard Android permissions necessary for basic networking:

| Permission | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| `android.permission.INTERNET` | Required to stream video content and load thumbnail images. |
| `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` | Required to detect network availability and prevent player errors when offline. |

---

## 4. Third-Party Services & Streaming

The App aggregates and plays publicly available Indian cinema and Hindi-dubbed films legally hosted on official YouTube channels (such as Goldmines, Shemaroo, Rajshri, Ultra, and Ishtar).

To deliver this content, the App interacts with the following third-party services:

### A. YouTube API Services & Embedded Player
- The video playback functionality is powered by the embedded YouTube Player (`android-youtube-player`).
- Video streaming and video data retrieval are subject to YouTube's terms and Google's data practices.
- By using video playback within the App, you acknowledge and agree that your interaction with YouTube is governed by:
  - **YouTube Terms of Service:** [https://www.youtube.com/t/terms](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms)
  - **Google Privacy Policy:** [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### B. Image Loading (YouTube CDN)
- Poster and thumbnail images are retrieved directly from YouTube's public media servers (`img.youtube.com`) using the Coil image loader.

### C. Advertising (Google AdMob)
- The App includes architecture for optional Google AdMob banner advertisements. 
- When advertisements are active, third-party ad vendors, including Google, may use cookies or mobile device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID / GAID) to serve ads based on non-identifiable usage statistics and device characteristics in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.
- You can manage or reset your device advertising identifier anytime in your Android settings under **Settings > Google > Ads**.

## 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Family Policy Compliance)

Hindi Cinema Movie Zone is a general audience entertainment application. 
- We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age limit in your jurisdiction).
- Since no registration or personal data collection takes place, no child data is ever collected, stored, or processed.
- If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided any personal information, please contact us immediately so we can investigate.

## 6. Data Security and Retention

Because all user preferences and Watchlist records are stored strictly on the local client device:
- No user data is stored on remote servers managed by the developer.
- Your data is protected by Android's sandbox security model.
- Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all local data and preferences associated with it.
