Privacy Policy

Application: Hindi Cinema Movie Zone (com.southaction.hindimovies)
Effective Date: September 20, 2026 | Last Updated: September 20, 2026
Summary: Hindi Cinema Movie Zone is built as a zero-friction movie discovery and streaming curation tool. We do not require an account, we do not track your personal identity, and we do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII).

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

We respect your right to privacy. When using our App:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, billing details, or physical addresses.
  • No Accounts or Logins: No registration, sign-up, or authentication is required to access any feature in the App.
  • No Sensitive Permissions: The App never requests access to your Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Call Logs, SMS, Photo Gallery, or Precise GPS Location.

2. Information Handled Locally on Your Device

Local Watchlist ("My List")

When you save movies to "My List" (Watchlist), this data is stored solely on your device using Android's local SQLite database (Room). This information never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any external server or database. You can remove items individually within the App, or reset all local data through Android Settings > Apps > Hindi Cinema Movie Zone > Storage > Clear Data.

3. Device Permissions Used

Permission Purpose
android.permission.INTERNET Required to stream movie videos and load promotional poster artwork.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Required to monitor network status and prevent playback errors when offline.

4. Third-Party Services & Streaming

The App aggregates and streams legal, publicly available Indian cinema hosted on official YouTube channels (such as Goldmines, Shemaroo, Rajshri, Ultra, and Ishtar).

A. YouTube API Services & Embedded Player

Video playback is facilitated through the embedded YouTube player. Interaction with the video player is subject to Google and YouTube terms:

  • YouTube Terms of Service
  • Google Privacy Policy

B. Image Loading (YouTube CDN)

Movie thumbnails and artwork are retrieved directly from YouTube's public media servers (img.youtube.com).

C. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App contains architecture for optional Google AdMob banner ads. When ads are enabled, third-party advertising partners may use device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID / GAID) to serve relevant advertisements in accordance with Google Play Developer Policies. You can reset your ad ID anytime in your device settings under Settings > Google > Ads.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

The App is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. Because our service requires no registration or data submission, no personal data from any user—including children—is gathered.

6. Data Security and Retention

Because all user data (such as your Watchlist) is saved strictly on your local device, it is secured by Android's application sandbox. We do not maintain remote servers or databases with your information. Uninstalling the App immediately removes all stored data.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
